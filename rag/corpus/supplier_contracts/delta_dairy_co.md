# Supplier Contract — Delta Dairy Co.

Supplier ID: 2 | Contact: sales@deltadairy.com | Category: Dairy

## Section 1 — Delivery Terms

### 1.1 Cold Chain Requirement
All dairy deliveries must arrive in a refrigerated vehicle maintaining 0-4°C
throughout transit. A delivery arriving above 4°C, verified by the
receiving temperature check (Food Safety & Storage Policy, Section 2.1),
is grounds for automatic rejection regardless of printed expiry date.

### 1.2 Delivery Window
Standard delivery window is confirmed at order time. Unlike Nile Fresh
Produce, Delta Dairy Co. does not commit to a fixed late-delivery penalty
schedule — late deliveries are handled case-by-case via the escalation
contact in Section 4.

## Section 2 — Quality Guarantee

### 2.1 Milk
Delta Dairy Co. guarantees a minimum 10-day shelf life from delivery date
for whole milk, provided cold-chain requirements (Section 1.1) were met on
arrival and storage complies with Food Safety & Storage Policy Section 1.2.

### 2.2 Feta Cheese
Feta delivered in brine carries a 45-day guaranteed shelf life from
delivery. Vacuum-packed feta (non-brine) carries only a 21-day guarantee.
Copperleaf staff should confirm packaging type at receiving, since this
materially changes expected shelf life (cross-reference Food Safety &
Storage Policy Section 1.2).

## Section 3 — Return & Remediation Policy

### 3.1 Damaged or Underperforming Product
Claims for product that spoiled before its guaranteed shelf-life window
(Section 2) must be filed within 5 business days of the spoilage being
discovered, with the relevant `item_id` and transaction reference.

### 3.2 Credit Terms
Approved claims are credited against the next invoice rather than refunded
directly.

## Section 4 — Contact & Escalation

### 4.1 Standard Contact
sales@deltadairy.com for ordering and routine questions.

### 4.2 Escalation
Cold-chain failures (Section 1.1) or repeated early-spoilage claims (Section
3.1) should be escalated by phone rather than email, given the food-safety
urgency of a broken cold chain.
