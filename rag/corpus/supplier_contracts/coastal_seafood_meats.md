# Supplier Contract — Coastal Seafood & Meats

Supplier ID: 3 | Contact: orders@coastalmeats.com | Category: Protein (seafood, poultry, red meat)

## Section 1 — Delivery Terms

### 1.1 Cold Chain Requirement
All protein deliveries must arrive at or below 2°C, verified at receiving
per Food Safety & Storage Policy Section 2.1. Seafood specifically must
arrive packed on ice with a visible ice log/temperature strip; deliveries
without this are grounds for automatic rejection.

### 1.2 Delivery Window
Confirmed at order time. A delivery is late if it arrives more than 12
hours after the confirmed `expected_delivery` date — a tighter window than
Nile Fresh Produce's 24-hour standard (Section 1.2 of that contract),
reflecting the shorter shelf life of this category.

## Section 2 — Quality Guarantee

### 2.1 Seafood
Coastal Seafood & Meats guarantees seafood is usable for 48 hours from
delivery, matching the Food Safety & Storage Policy's Section 1.3 guidance.
No guarantee applies beyond 48 hours regardless of storage conditions.

### 2.2 Poultry & Red Meat
Guaranteed shelf life is 4 days from delivery for poultry (e.g., chicken
breast) and 5 days for red meat (e.g., ribeye), provided storage complies
with Food Safety & Storage Policy Section 1.3.

## Section 3 — Return & Remediation Policy

### 3.1 Standard Return Window
Damaged-in-delivery claims must be filed within 12 hours of receiving for
seafood (matching its faster spoilage profile) and within 24 hours for
poultry/red meat.

### 3.2 Repeated Cold-Chain Failures
Two or more cold-chain failures (Section 1.1) within any rolling 30-day
window trigger a mandatory supplier review call and give Copperleaf the
right to source the affected item category from an alternate supplier for
up to 60 days without breaching any exclusivity terms.

## Section 4 — Contact & Escalation

### 4.1 Standard Contact
orders@coastalmeats.com for ordering; reference the affected `item_id` and
`order_id` for any claim.

### 4.2 Escalation
Cold-chain failures (Section 3.2) should be escalated by phone the same day
they're discovered, given the higher spoilage risk of this category
compared to produce or dairy.
